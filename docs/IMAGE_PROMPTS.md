# Don Corte Fino — AI Image Prompts

Every product and section image on the site is AI-generated in **one consistent style**. Each prompt below is the **style base** with the **subject** filled in.

## Style base (add to every prompt)

```
Ultra-realistic high-end food photography, Wagyu x Angus crossbreed beef,
deep ruby-red meat with fine, evenly distributed creamy-white intramuscular
marbling (BMS 5-7, realistic, not extreme A5), natural muscle grain visible.
Pure black background (#1D1D1C), low-key lighting, single soft key light from
upper left, subtle warm rim light, gentle falloff into darkness. Matte black
slate or dark charred-wood board. Sparse coarse sea-salt flakes, a single
sprig of fresh rosemary. Shot on a medium-format camera, 100mm macro lens,
f/5.6, razor-sharp focus on the meat, shallow depth of field. Luxury butcher
editorial, moody, elegant, minimal, rich contrast, true-to-life colour.
```

**Negative prompt (add if the tool supports one):**
```
text, letters, logo, watermark, label, hands, people, plastic wrap, styrofoam
tray, cartoon, illustration, CGI look, oversaturated, neon, blue cast, bright
background, white background, cluttered props, deformed meat, melted fat,
unrealistic marbling, extra bones, blurry, low resolution
```

**Shapes:** hero and raw shots 4:5 (1600×2000 or larger) · marbling close-ups 1:1 · section backgrounds 16:9 (2560×1440 or larger).

## Quality check (every image)
- [ ] Marbling looks real (fine and natural, not plastic or painted on)
- [ ] The cut's shape matches its real anatomy (bone position, fat cap)
- [ ] Background is truly black, no colour cast
- [ ] No text, logos, hands or odd objects
- [ ] Colour matches the rest of the set
- [ ] Sharp at full size, no AI artefacts (melted edges, repeated textures)
